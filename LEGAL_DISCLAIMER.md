# Legal disclaimer

**immigration-ops provides general information. It is not legal or immigration advice.**

## What this project is

A tool that compiles published immigration rules from public official sources and applies
them to the information you give it. Every rule it uses cites the official page it came from
and the date it was last checked.

## What it is not

- It is not a lawyer, a registered migration agent or a licensed immigration consultant.
- It does not assess your individual case, predict a decision, or tell you what you should do.
- It does not submit applications or communicate with any authority on your behalf.
- It is not affiliated with any government.

## Limits you should know

- **Rules change.** A rule can change the day after it was checked. Reports show the date
  each source was verified and flag sources that are overdue for review.
- **Published rules are not the whole picture.** Officers have discretion, quotas fill, and
  individual circumstances matter in ways no general tool can see.
- **Your inputs decide the output.** An assumed or mistaken fact gives a wrong result.
- **AI can be wrong.** The explanations and plans are written by an AI model reading the
  engine's output. The numbers come from tested code and sourced data, but the prose around
  them can still contain errors.

**Always confirm on the official source before you spend money, sit a test, resign from a
job or submit an application.**

## Regulated advice

In several countries only licensed or registered people may give immigration advice,
especially for a fee. Each country pack links the official register of such advisers. Use
that register to check anyone who offers to advise or represent you. Nobody associated with
this project is acting as your adviser.
