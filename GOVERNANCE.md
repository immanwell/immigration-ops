# Governance

immigration-ops is maintainer-led. [@immanwell](https://github.com/immanwell) is the
maintainer and makes final decisions. The aim is to share that responsibility country by
country as people earn it.

## How decisions are made

| Decision | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Architecture: the engine, the pack format, the data contract | Maintainer, after an issue or discussion |
| A rule update with an official source | The country maintainer, or the maintainer |
| A new route or origin pack | The country maintainer and the maintainer |
| A new country | The maintainer, once someone commits to maintaining it |
| Docs and wording fixes | Any maintainer |

## Roles

**Contributor.** Anyone with a merged pull request.

**Country maintainer.** Owns one country's route packs or one origin pack. Reviews data pull
requests for it and handles its freshness issues. Listed in
[CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Earned by building or substantially maintaining that
country's pack and showing care with sources.

**Maintainer.** Reviews engine and mode changes, cuts releases, and resolves disagreements.

A country maintainer who is inactive for six months with open freshness issues may be asked
whether they wish to continue, and replaced if there is no reply.

## What the project will not do

These hold regardless of who maintains it:

- State a rule without an official source.
- Give advice to an individual, or accept payment to do so.
- Accept advertising, referral arrangements or sponsored placement for agents, consultancies
  or employers.
- Collect user data.

## Changing this document

Open an issue. The maintainer decides after discussion.
