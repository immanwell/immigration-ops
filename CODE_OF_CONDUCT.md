# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel used for the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. Many people here are under real pressure about their future.
- Criticise ideas, data and code. Never people.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, including on the basis of nationality,
  origin, immigration status, religion, race, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
- Do not post anyone's personal or case details, including your own documents.

## Specific to this project

- **No selling.** Do not use project spaces to advertise visa agents, consultancies, job
  "sponsorship" offers or paid services.
- **No encouragement to mislead.** Suggestions to misrepresent facts to an immigration
  authority, or to get around a rule, will be removed.
- **No individual predictions presented as fact.** Share what the published rules say and
  where they say it.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer privately, through the contact details at
<https://em.is-a.dev>, or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment. Reports are
kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content, and may warn or ban a participant, in
line with the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
